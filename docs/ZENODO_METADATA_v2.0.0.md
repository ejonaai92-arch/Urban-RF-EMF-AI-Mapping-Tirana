**Description:**
Code, data and figures accompanying the revised manuscript "Random Forest Prediction of Urban Radiofrequency Electromagnetic Field Exposure from Multiband Field Measurements in Tirana". The record contains 171 georeferenced multi-band RF-EMF spot measurements (Narda SRM-3006, 700 MHz–6 GHz) from Mustafa Kemal Atatürk Square and Karl Topia Square, Tirana, Albania, the full spectra and field records, the coordinates of the five surveyed base stations, and Python scripts reproducing all analyses of the revised manuscript: Random Forest single-split, repeated cross-validation and bootstrap evaluation, spatial-only and multiple-linear-regression baselines, leave-one-out comparison of interpolation methods including ordinary kriging, out-of-fold hotspot agreement, learning curves, weekday/weekend sensitivity, FDD uplink share and noise-corrected exposure levels. Version 2.0.0 extends v1.0.0 (original submission), which is preserved unchanged in the v1_original folders.
